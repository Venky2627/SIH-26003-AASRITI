# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (Healthcare & Dementia Care Edition)

## 🩺 Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and clinically responsible community, we as contributors and maintainers of **SmritiSetu (SIH26003)** pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free and ethically grounded experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Because our application directly interfaces with **vulnerable elderly individuals diagnosed with dementia and cognitive impairment**, as well as their non-clinical family caregivers in underserved indigenous communities across the North Eastern Region of India, our ethical duties extend beyond standard software conduct into bioethical responsibilities:
1. **Respect for Cognitive Dignity**: We never design patterns, test cases, or documentation that trivialize, mock, or exploit cognitive decline.
2. **Clinical Humility**: We do not present experimental software heuristics as definitive diagnostic or therapeutic claims without empirical clinical validation.
3. **Cultural Sensitivity**: We actively honor and protect the indigenous cultural traditions, linguistic dialects, and customs of Assam, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, and Sikkim.

---

## 🌟 Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and architectural experiences.
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism from clinical mentors, caregivers, and fellow engineers.
* Focusing on what is best for the elderly end-user and the local caregiving collective.
* Showing empathy towards other community members and acknowledging the high stress of hackathon delivery.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical addresses or electronic addresses, without explicit permission.
* **Egregious Medical Violation**: Committing real patient medical records, raw audio recordings, or non-anonymized psychometric test results to Git history.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or healthcare setting.

## 🛡️ Scope & Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, project meetings, communication channels) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project leadership team via GitHub:
[https://github.com/TeamNameSIH/AI-Based-Cognitive-Gaming-Platform-for-Elderly-Dementia-Patients-in-NER/issues](https://github.com/TeamNameSIH/AI-Based-Cognitive-Gaming-Platform-for-Elderly-Dementia-Patients-in-NER/issues). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
