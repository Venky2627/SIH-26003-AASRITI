# AASRITI NORTH EASTERN HERITAGE DESIGN GUIDE
## `/UI_HERITAGE_GUIDE.md` — Authentic Regional Cultural Integration

> **GUIDING AXIOM**: Culture in AASRITI is not superficial wall decoration. Culture is **familiarity, emotional comfort, and cognitive grounding** for elders experiencing memory loss.

---

## 🧭 1. CORE PHILOSOPHIES OF HERITAGE DESIGN

1. **Subtle, Authentic, Contextual**:
   - Cultural elements must live in the margins, borders, activity content, and voice prompts.
   - **NEVER** cover an entire screen with full-bleed ethnic textile wallpapers that reduce contrast or cause visual confusion.
2. **Strict Regional Independence**:
   - **NEVER** homogenize the North East into one generic "tribal" or "folk" aesthetic.
   - Assam, Manipur, and Meghalaya have distinct languages, textiles, traditional architectures, and food cultures. They must be individually respected and identifiable.
3. **Decoupled from Language**:
   - A family may speak English or Hindi at home while identifying deeply with Assamese or Meitei cultural roots. Cultural theme packs are selected independently from the app UI language.
4. **Zero Stereotyping**:
   - Do not use caricatures, exaggerated tribal headgear, or ritualistic depictions out of context.
   - Use daily life objects: tea cups, woven baskets, handloom borders, river scenes, domestic garden plants.

---

## 🌾 2. ASSAM CULTURAL THEME PACK

### Approved Visual Motifs & Forms:
* **Gamosa Geometry**: The red-and-white *Phulam Gamosa* floral and diamond border motif used as an elegant 4dp–8dp top or bottom separator.
* **Jaapi Forms**: Stylized woven bamboo conical sunshade silhouettes used in onboarding illustrations and activity icons.
* **Muga Silk Warmth**: The golden sheen of natural Assam *Muga* silk (`Muga Gold` `#C88D34`) used for voice buttons and gentle achievements.
* **Tea Garden & Brahmaputra Landscapes**: Soft watercolor-style illustrations of rolling green tea bushes, country wooden boats, and misty river horizons.
* **Traditional Domestic Foods**: *Jolpan* (flattened rice with curd and jaggery), *Pitha* (rice cakes), *Kaji Nemu* (Assam lemon), *Bhogali Bihu* tea preparations.

### Forbidden Misrepresentations:
* ❌ Commercial tea-factory industrial imagery.
* ❌ Overwhelming full-screen red-and-white textile patterns that destroy WCAG text contrast.
* ❌ Conflating Bihu festival folk dance with everyday medical interactions.

---

## 🌺 3. MANIPUR CULTURAL THEME PACK

### Approved Visual Motifs & Forms:
* **Moirang Phee Chevron Borders**: The iconic triangular step-temple (*Yarong*) geometric border from traditional Manipuri handloom textiles, used as subtle divider lines.
* **Loktak Lake & Phumdi Floating Islands**: Tranquil water landscapes, circular fishing phumdis, and dugout canoe silhouettes for peaceful meditation and calming backgrounds.
* **Traditional Music (Pena)**: The *Pena* bowed lute instrument used as the visual anchor for auditory memory and folk music playback.
* **Manipuri Flora & Foods**: *Chak-hao* (purple black rice), fresh *Singju* salad, lotus flowers, traditional *Kangsoi* vegetable stew.
* **Restrained Palette**: Soft natural indigo, pale lotus pink, and unbleached off-white linen tones.

### Forbidden Misrepresentations:
* ❌ Garish theatrical dance makeup or dramatic mythological masks on patient screens.
* ❌ Confusing Meitei handloom with Assamese silk weaving.

---

## 🌲 4. MEGHALAYA CULTURAL THEME PACK

### Approved Visual Motifs & Forms:
* **Living Root Bridges & Rain Forests**: Architectural wonders of knotted Ficus elastica living roots, misty pine-covered ridges of Sohra/Shillong, and clean river streams.
* **Khasi & Garo Bamboo Basketry**: Clean vertical and criss-cross woven bamboo textures (*Khoh* basket weave) used in card frames.
* **Ryndia Eri Silk**: Peaceful earth tones inspired by non-violent Khasi *Ryndia* hand-spun wild silk in natural cream and herbal clay dyes.
* **Traditional Music (Duitara)**: The wooden four-stringed *Duitara* instrument representing storytelling and musical memory.
* **Traditional Foods**: Local orange groves, roasted corn, steamed rice preparations wrapped in banana leaves.

### Forbidden Misrepresentations:
* ❌ Depicting dangerous slippery waterfalls or storm rain that could induce anxiety in dementia patients.
* ❌ Using tourist postcard cliché graphics instead of quiet domestic life textures.

## 📊 5. HERITAGE DECORATION DENSITY MATRIX

Cultural elements must be applied with varying restraint depending on the operational gravity of the screen:

| Screen Type | Heritage Density | Approved Heritage Elements |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Welcome & Splash** | **HIGH (30%)** | Full regional welcome greeting, subtle textile border, landscape illustration. |
| **Memory Garden** | **HIGH (30%)** | Handcrafted photo album frame, regional music audio, family relationship labels. |
| **Patient Home** | **MODERATE (15%)** | Regional greeting honorific (*Aita*, *Kaka*, *Mei*), small textile separator, local time-of-day metaphor. |
| **Cognitive Games** | **MODERATE (15%)** | Culturally authentic stimuli (Joha rice, Kaji nemu, handloom shawls, market stalls). |
| **Caregiver Tools** | **LOW (5%)** | Small brand accent, localized patient names, subtle notification icons. |
| **Doctor / Clinical** | **MINIMAL (<2%)** | Standardized professional layout, patient demographic tags, clean high-contrast data charts. |
